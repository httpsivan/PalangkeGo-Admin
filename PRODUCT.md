# Product

## Register

product

## Users

MEPO (Market Enterprise Promotion Office) officers and market administrators
for the Naga City public market. Desktop-first (Flutter Windows) with a web
fallback; used all day at an office desk for vendor oversight. Primary jobs:
review and approve vendor KYC applications, verify annual license renewals,
block/unblock accounts, publish market-wide announcements, and audit market
revenue — each decision carries administrative weight and an audit trail.

## Product Purpose

The PalengkeGo Admin portal is the operations console of a three-part
marketplace platform (customer app, vendor app, admin portal) sharing one
Firebase/Firestore backend with trusted callables. Success = an officer can
process a KYC application in under two minutes with full confidence in the
data, every privileged action is auditable, and nothing fabricated is ever
shown as real market data.

## Brand Personality

Civic-professional: calm, authoritative, institutional. The interface earns
vendor trust by looking like it belongs to the city — not to a startup.
Three words: steady, precise, trustworthy.

## Anti-references

- SaaS-template feel: gradient accents, glassmorphism, floating stat cards,
  hero-metric pages.
- Toy-like playfulness: oversized illustration, bouncy motion, decoration
  competing with data. Tables and decisions ARE the product.

## Design Principles

1. **The decision is the hero** — every screen optimizes the path from
   "open" to "informed decision recorded"; nothing decorative stands between
   the officer and the record.
2. **Server truth only** — if a panel has no live data source, it shows an
   honest empty state, never seeded fiction.
3. **Audit-grade clarity** — status, actor, and time are always legible;
   no color-only signals; dense tables stay readable.
4. **Quiet density** — compact professional spacing, strong typographic
   hierarchy, motion limited to purposeful transitions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Stricter-than-AA: body text contrast targets WCAG AAA (7:1). Full keyboard
navigation, reduced-motion support, and no meaning conveyed by color alone.
